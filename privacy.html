01 / Scope

About this notice.

This notice describes Boomito for iPhone and iPad and this informational website. Core gameplay and locally saved progress work on the device. Advertising features require a network connection.

02 / App data

Stored locally.

Boomito saves campaign progress and sound and haptic preferences in local device storage. The app keeps a local recovery copy of campaign progress to help restore it if the primary saved entry is damaged.

These records are used to resume play and remember your settings. Boomito does not provide an account or cross-device progress sync, and the current app does not send these records to a Boomito server.

Development builds can create local research or performance files when specific developer-only tools are used. Those tools are not part of normal player use and the files are not uploaded by the app.

03 / Advertising

Ads and an optional rescue.

Boomito uses Google Mobile Ads SDK 13.9.0. After at least 180 seconds of active gameplay, an interstitial ad may appear at an eligible transition following an exact result. Time in menus, results, the background or an ad does not count toward that interval.

From Level 15, an eligible near miss may offer one optional rewarded rescue. If the reward is earned, the level is recorded as an assisted completion and play continues. You may skip the offer and retry normally, and assisted levels can be replayed for an exact completion. Ads are subject to availability.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, non-user-related crash logs, performance information, device identifiers, advertising data and product interactions such as launches, taps or video views. These may be used for advertising, measurement, analytics, service performance and related purposes. Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is available only when you authorize tracking; Google may use other app- or developer-bounded identifiers. See Google’s iOS data-disclosure guidance and Privacy Policy.

Boomito does not add a separate first-party analytics service, player account or first-party cloud progress service.

04 / Consent and tracking

Choices before ad requests.

Boomito uses Google User Messaging Platform SDK 3.1.0. The European regulations message is configured and published in AdMob. At each launch, the app requests updated consent information and presents that privacy message when required. Ads are requested only after the platform reports that requests are allowed.

After that consent step, iOS may ask for permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites when the system status is not yet determined. Refusing this permission does not block play. Without authorization, the IDFA is not available to Boomito or the advertising SDK.

Where required, Privacy choices is available from Settings & Help so you can revisit the Google-managed options. Settings & Help also links to this policy at boomito-support.vercel.app/privacy.html. You can manage the separate iOS tracking permission in iOS Settings. The exact ads served—personalized, non-personalized, limited or technical delivery—depend on region, available inventory and your choices. See Google’s UMP documentation.

05 / This website

A static information site.

The website files add no analytics, advertising, contact form, cookies, or browser storage. Its images and styles are served with the site.

The site is prepared for hosting on Vercel. Vercel may process website traffic information such as IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser or device information, request details, diagnostics and logs to provide and protect its services. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice.

06 / Your control

Your device, your settings.

You can change sound and haptic preferences inside Boomito, revisit available privacy choices from Settings & Help, and manage iOS tracking permission in iOS Settings. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove locally saved progress, assisted-completion records, preferences and locally stored consent choices. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and backup settings.

07 / Contact and updates

Details to confirm before launch.

The final policy must identify the publisher or data controller and provide a privacy contact method. Once a support method is confirmed, this section should also explain how support messages and attachments are handled.

This notice may be updated if the app, website, advertising configuration or connected services change. The date above identifies this draft’s latest review.
