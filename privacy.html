01 / Scope

About this notice.

This notice describes the current Boomito iOS app and this informational website. Boomito is a native game for iPhone and iPad. The app can be played offline.

02 / App data

Stored locally.

Boomito saves campaign progress and sound and haptic preferences in local device storage. The app keeps a local recovery copy of campaign progress to help restore it if the primary saved entry is damaged.

These records are used to resume play and remember your settings. Boomito does not provide an account or cross-device progress sync, and the current app does not send these records to a Boomito server.

Development builds can create local research or performance files when specific developer-only tools are used. Those tools are not part of normal player use and the files are not uploaded by the app.

03 / Services not used

No ads. No analytics. No tracking.

The current app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking permission request, third-party sign-in, in-app purchases, cloud account, or network service. It does not request access to contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

04 / This website

A static information site.

The website files add no analytics, advertising, contact form, cookies, or browser storage. Its images and styles are served with the site.

The hosting provider has not yet been selected. Before publication, this section should name the provider and accurately describe any standard request information it processes to deliver and protect the site.

05 / Your control

Your device, your settings.

You can change sound and haptic preferences inside Boomito. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove locally saved progress and preferences. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and backup settings.

06 / Contact and updates

Details to confirm before launch.

The final policy must identify the publisher or data controller and provide a privacy contact method. Once a support method is confirmed, this section should also explain how support messages and attachments are handled.

This notice may be updated if the app, website or connected services change. The date above identifies this draft’s latest review.

Publication checklist

Add the confirmed publisher identity, privacy/support address, hosting provider and public site URL. Recheck the shipped app build before publishing this policy.
