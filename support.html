01 / How to play

One swipe. One answer.

  1. Read the target at the top of the screen.
  2. Start on the blue floor and keep one finger down.
  3. Touching a ball removes it immediately. Every color counts as one.
  4. Release when you think you have the exact target. Your count appears only after release.

There is no penalty for retrying. An exact answer unlocks the next level.

02 / Walls and edges

Keep holding.

Coral walls do not remove balls. If your finger crosses a wall or leaves the tray, keep holding: collection resumes when your finger reaches the blue floor again. Releasing anywhere locks the answer.

03 / Progress

Saved on this device.

Boomito stores your campaign progress locally. Continue returns to the first unfinished level after a replay, and the Levels view lets you revisit unlocked levels.

There is no Boomito account or cross-device sync. Deleting the app or all of its local data may remove progress.

04 / Settings

Choose the feedback you want.

Open the gear button to turn sound and haptics on or off separately. These preferences are saved on your device. The same screen includes the in-app play guide.

05 / Troubleshooting

Quick answers.

Why can’t I see my count while swiping?

That is the core rule. Boomito keeps the count hidden until you release, so the challenge is to feel the quantity rather than follow a live total.

What happens if I open a menu mid-swipe?

An unfinished attempt is cancelled without scoring and restarts cleanly when you return. A result that has already been committed remains locked.

Can I jump to any level?

You can revisit any level you have unlocked. Browsing later pages does not unlock their levels.

Does Boomito require an internet connection?

No. The current game has no network services and can be played offline.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, the level number, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.
